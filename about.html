About Me

I am a BS-MS dual degree student in Chemistry at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Mohali, India. My academic focus is on quantum mechanics, molecular spectroscopy, and photocatalysis — with particular interest in how light-matter interactions drive chemical transformations in sustainable energy systems.

This site documents my research interests, scholarly activities, and educational outreach in the physical sciences. It is intended as an academic portfolio for graduate admissions and research collaboration.
