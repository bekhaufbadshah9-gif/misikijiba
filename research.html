Research

Quantum Mechanics & Spectroscopy

Research Statement

My research lies at the intersection of quantum mechanics, spectroscopy, and photocatalysis. I am interested in understanding how light-matter interactions drive chemical transformations in sustainable energy systems, and how spectroscopic techniques can reveal the underlying quantum dynamics of functional materials.

Specifically, I aim to investigate: (1) the role of vibrational coherence in photocatalytic efficiency, (2) the design of earth-abundant photocatalysts for solar fuel generation, and (3) the development of time-resolved spectroscopic methods for probing non-equilibrium molecular dynamics.

Research Interests

Core areas of inquiry and methodological focus

Quantum Mechanics

Wavefunction dynamics, uncertainty relations, and quantum coherence in molecular systems. Applications to quantum sensing and quantum information processing.

Spectroscopy

Raman, IR, UV-Vis, and time-resolved spectroscopy for probing molecular structure, vibrational dynamics, and reaction intermediates.

Photocatalysis

Light-driven chemical reactions for sustainable energy conversion, solar fuel generation, and environmental remediation using earth-abundant materials.

Material Chemistry

Design and synthesis of functional materials with tailored optical and electronic properties for photocatalysis and energy applications.

Computational Chemistry

Density functional theory (DFT), molecular dynamics, and quantum chemical modeling to predict and interpret experimental observations.

Sustainable Energy

Artificial photosynthesis, CO2 reduction, water splitting, and solar energy conversion through molecular and materials engineering.
